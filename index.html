About Zara'a Agro Holdings

Zara'a Agro Holdings is a vertically integrated agricultural holding company headquartered in Tunisia, operating across four essential sectors that form the backbone of the national agricultural economy.

The group brings together pesticide distribution, grain trading, diesel supply, and field agri-services under one unified holding structure — creating synergies that benefit our clients, partners, and the Tunisian agricultural community.

Built on a foundation of trust and Tunisian heritage, our ambition extends beyond our borders while remaining firmly rooted in the land we serve.
